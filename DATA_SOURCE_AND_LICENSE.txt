TARGET NOTE: BigSolDB v2.2 LogS(mol/L) is recalculated using pure-solvent density. Its denominator is litres of solvent, not litres of solution. The model target is the molecule-wise median of that converted reported value. pH and solid-form effects are not modeled. These are not newly performed measurements.
